So, next time your CPC jumps, run this checklist. Minute one: check C P A and ROAS. If they're fine, stop there. Minutes two to four: change history. Did anyone change budgets, targets, or bid strategy? Minutes four to six: segment by campaign, and find where the increase is actually happening. Minutes six to eight: auction insights, for new competitors. Minutes eight to ten: Quality Score and its history. Minutes ten to thirteen: search terms, sorted by cost. And minutes thirteen to fifteen: conversion tracking, compared against your real sales. Last of all, compare the same period last year to rule out seasonality.
