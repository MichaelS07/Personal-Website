Cause number two. Your Quality Score dropped. Remember, it's the number on the bottom of the formula. When it falls, your CPC rises, even if nothing else changes. To check it, go to your Keywords view, and add four columns: Quality Score, Expected click through rate, Ad relevance, and Landing page experience. Then add the historical versions of those columns, so you can see when it changed. Each part gets a rating of below average, average, or above average. That tells you exactly what to fix. Below average expected CTR? Your ads aren't compelling. Test new headlines, and add sitelinks and callouts. Below average ad relevance? Your ad groups are too broad. Split them up so the ad matches the search. Below average landing page? The page is slow, or doesn't match what the ad promised. Check it on mobile. A page that takes five seconds to load is costing you money on every click.
