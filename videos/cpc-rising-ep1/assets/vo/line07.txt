Cause number three. Your own bidding. Sometimes the CPC increase is self inflicted. Go to Tools, then Change history, and filter by bid and budget changes. Look for three things. One, a budget increase on Maximise conversions. With no target set, Google will spend that extra money by bidding into more expensive auctions. Two, a raised target C P A, or a lowered target ROAS. You've told Google it's allowed to pay more, so it does. Three, a switch to target impression share, especially absolute top of page. That strategy chases position, not profit, and it'll pay whatever it takes. Also check the bid strategy status. If it says learning, CPCs often jump around for about a week. The fix: set realistic targets, change them in small steps of around fifteen to twenty percent, and give each change time before you judge it.
