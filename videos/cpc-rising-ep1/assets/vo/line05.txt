Cause number one. More competition. A new competitor enters your auctions, or an existing one raises their budget. The advertiser below you gets stronger, so you pay more. Here's where to check. Go to Insights and reports, then Auction insights. Set the date range to compare this period with the last one. Look for a new name in the list, or a competitor whose overlap rate or outranking share jumped. If someone went from twenty percent overlap to sixty, that's your answer. The fix is not to get into a bidding war. You'll both lose, and Google wins. Instead, raise your Quality Score, which we'll cover next, so you beat them without paying more. Find where you still win, by device, location or time of day, and push budget there. And if they're bidding on your brand name, protect your brand campaign. It's usually the cheapest traffic you'll ever buy.
