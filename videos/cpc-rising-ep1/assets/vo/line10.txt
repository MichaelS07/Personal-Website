Cause number six. And this one catches a lot of people. Your conversion tracking is broken. Smart Bidding learns from your conversion data. If that data is wrong, it bids wrong. The most common example is a conversion that fires twice. Google thinks each click is worth double, so it bids higher and your CPC climbs. The same thing happens when a low value action, like a page view, is set as a primary conversion. To check, go to Goals, then Conversions summary. Look at every primary action. Is the status recording properly? Do the numbers match what your shop or CRM says actually happened? If Google says forty sales and Shopify says twenty, you've found your problem. Fix the tag, remove duplicates, and set only real business outcomes as primary.
