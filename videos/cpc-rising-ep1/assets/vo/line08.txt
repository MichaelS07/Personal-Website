Cause number four. Your keywords are matching expensive searches. Broad match, and Performance Max, decide for themselves which searches you show up for. Over time, they can drift into queries that are more competitive, and often less relevant. You bid on "running shoes," and suddenly you're paying for "Nike Pegasus 41 sale," competing with every big retailer in the country. To check, open Insights and reports, then Search terms. Sort by cost, and look at the top fifty. For each one, ask: would I have chosen this keyword myself? The fix is to add negative keywords for anything irrelevant, move your best converting searches into their own exact or phrase match keywords, and make this a weekly habit, not a once a year clean up.
