## Intent

Episode 1 of a weekly NovaReach YouTube series: one digital marketing topic per
week, explained properly ("actually explains stuff and isn't useless"). This one
covers how to work out why your Google Ads CPC is rising and how to fix it.
Animated faceless explainer built around a mock Google Ads interface. Practical,
calm, no hype. Every cause gets a symptom, the exact place to check it in Google
Ads, and the fix.

## Customizations

- AI voiceover (Kokoro, offline) with synced on-screen captions.
- Reusable scene templates (cause card, UI zoom, checklist) so later episodes can
  reuse the frames with a new script.
