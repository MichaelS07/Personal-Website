Google AI Mode's Zero-Click Problem: What It Means for Your Paid Search Strategy

May 4, 2026 • Paid Media • 7 min read

Google AI Mode has 75 million daily users and ads in 25.5% of AI results, but Seer Interactive found 93% of AI Mode sessions end without a click. This post covers what that means for branded search, informational queries, Shopping Direct Offers, and five actions: attribution, match types, product-feed quality, first-party measurement, and branded revenue vs traffic.
