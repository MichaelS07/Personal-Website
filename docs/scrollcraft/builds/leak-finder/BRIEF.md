# Leak finder: NovaReach homepage rebuild

Interviewed in part (four questions, answered 16 Oct 2026). The remaining topics are authored decisions under the direction "use the leak-funnel idea", and are marked as such.

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe.** User answer: "Keep dark + emerald." Authored: analyst's instrument, calm, exact, a bit dry. References: a flight-deck status panel, a bank statement, Bloomberg terminal type density (not its colours).
2. **The scroll journey.** User picked the leak-funnel rebuild: "the funnel drains as you scroll, each section patches a leak, and it ends at the audit form." Authored sequence: funnel runs, leak found, leak patched, which leak is yours, the 90-day fix log, notes, pricing, the person, the input.
3. **Energy curve.** Authored: calm open, rising unease while the funnel drains, a held quiet when the leak is named, the release when it seals, then steady and practical to the end.
4. **Feeling, and the one moment.** See the feeling curve and peak below.
5. **What no other site does.** Authored from the brief: the page is the audit. You watch a sample store leak, the fix closes the leak, and then the same instrument runs on your numbers.
6. **Range.** User answer: dark + emerald. Interpreted as "dense" leaning, inside a dark ground. No gradient text, no glows, no constellation background.
7. **One world or distinct scenes.** Authored: distinct sections on one surface. The page is one working instrument, not a camera flight.
8. **Assets.** User answer: "Code-drawn only." No photos, no generated imagery, no API spend. Everything is SVG, canvas and markup.

## Grammar

Live surface (uniqueness.md §2.3). The demo is the argument: Michael sells an audit, so the page runs one. Every number on the instrument is computed in the page from a labelled sample store or from the visitor's own inputs. Nothing is a painted dashboard.

Why the others lost: filmic one-shot and continuous world need footage (none, by choice). Chaptered editorial wants a paper title page and running-text CTA, which fights a lead-gen form and the dark brief. Typographic poster and rhythmic cutlist suit energy brands, not an analyst. Gallery has no catalogue to show. Split stage was close (leaking vs fixed), but pricing, notes and about cannot carry two columns the whole way down.

## The peak

"The red leak at checkout sealed up and the revenue number jumped, then it let me put in my own numbers." Lives in act 1 (phase 1d), which has the largest span on the page. Phase 1c before it is the authored silence: no motion, one sentence.

## Tell-someone sentence

It's the site where you watch a store's checkout leak, see the fix close it, and then run the same sums on your own shop.

## Signature move

The patch. A scroll-driven seal on the leaking stage that stops the spill and recomputes the funnel, followed by a live handle (checkout completion) and two inputs (sessions, order value) the visitor operates. A status bar along the bottom carries the current what-if all the way down the page.

## Honesty rules for this build

- The sample store is labelled "Sample store" on the instrument at all times.
- The 50% to 60% patch is a what-if the visitor can move, never a client result.
- No count-up animations on numbers. Numbers change only because the model recomputed.

## Authored silence

Phase 1c (roughly 0.46 to 0.58 of act 1's progress) is deliberate stillness, not dead scroll.
